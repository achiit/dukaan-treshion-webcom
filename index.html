About the shop

I started this shop so nobody leaves with a bad fit.

When I was young, my uncle used to take me along when he bought cloth for the family. He would hold every piece to the light and say, "If it does not feel right in the hand, it will not feel right on the body."

In 2019 I opened Treshion at Shop No 9 in Sri Vinayak Market Complex, with that one rule. Come in, touch the fabric, try it on in front of the mirror, and take your time. I would rather lose a sale than sell a shirt that pulls at the shoulder.

Today college students and young professionals from Chandrasekharpur and nearby come back for every new drop. That, along with our 4.52 on Google, is what I am proudest of.

  • Honest fit advice, even if it means a smaller bill
  • Clear tagged prices, no surprise at the counter
  • Trial and mirror on the shop floor, no pressure to buy
  • New styles on the racks often
  • Quick WhatsApp replies from the shop itself
